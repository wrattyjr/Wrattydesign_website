Kruise
Client

Ignite Innovations

Service

Branding, Packaging

Category

Packaging

Kruise is a car air freshener brand built to stop people at the checkout. The vent clip blister card leads with a chunky, sticker-outlined wordmark over a retro sunburst swirl that switches colour scent by scent, so the range reads as one bold block on the peg while each flavour still pops on its own. Names like Cherry Bombastic, Tsunami Breeze and Black Knight are paired with illustrated icons and a TRY ME callout, giving a category usually dressed in pine trees and chrome a candy-aisle personality.
